# `FilterDesign/data/` — what each file is and how to read it

Generated by `../prepare_filter_data.py`. **Nothing here is a new measurement** —
every file is a re-expression of a capture already committed under
`Experiment_SMAThermalCharacterization/data/raw/` or `Experiment_RNoise/out/scope/`,
with the corrections that a naive read gets wrong applied once, here.
`index.csv` is the machine-readable version of this table.

## The four channels are two different converters

| channel | ADC | pin / input | rate | source impedance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| laser `src=1` | ADS1263 (external Δ-Σ), Sinc3 | AIN4/5 diff | **400 SPS real** (496 Hz read) | Keyence IL-030 amp out |
| load `src=2` | ADS1263, Sinc3 | AIN2/3 diff | **400 SPS real** | LCA-9PC amp out |
| V_sma `src=3` | H7 on-chip 16-bit SAR | **A0**, via 10k/10k divider | ~1 kHz | **5 kΩ** |
| I_sense `src=4` | H7 on-chip 16-bit SAR | **A1**, INA296A OUT | ~1 kHz | low (op-amp) |

The ADS1263 pair has a Sinc3 decimation filter in front of it; A0/A1 have
**nothing** — no analog filter, and `n_avg` is a boxcar over consecutive
conversions, which is a decimator, not an anti-alias filter.

---

## A — ADS1263 noise floor, rig at rest

`A_idle_laser.csv`, `A_idle_load.csv` — 60 s, SMA disarmed and disconnected,
nothing actuating. Source: `noise_20260728_125440_quiet_baseline/h7_quiet.csv`.

Columns: `t_s, dt_s, volts, disp_um|force_mN, raw_code, seq, n_held`.

This is the noise the filter is being asked to remove on those two channels.

## B — the same, load cell shorted at the amplifier input

`B_shorted_laser.csv`, `B_shorted_load.csv` — SIG+ shorted to SIG− at the
amplifier terminals; bridge still powered and in circuit, so the sensor's
*signal* is removed but excitation, amplifier, cable and ADC are untouched.
Source: `noise_20260728_shorted_input_test/h7_shorted.csv`. Same columns as A.

A vs B is the **ceiling on any front-end fix for the load cell**: the
downstream half is 3.11 mV of the 4.76 mV total, so removing everything before
the amplifier caps you at ~1.5×.

## C — V_sma / I_sense at the deployed ~1 kHz, six operating points

`C_<time>_<label>_vi.csv` — columns `t_s, dt_s, v_sma_V, i_A, r_ohm, p_W, dac_code, seq`.
`C_<time>_<label>_laser.csv` / `_load.csv` — the ADS1263 channels from the *same*
capture, i.e. with the drive live (drive-EMI feedthrough), same columns as A/B.

Source: `data/raw/troubleshoot/isense_20260728_*`. Conditions span SMA
disconnected, constant-current at 100 / 106 / 155 mA, and one `cccycle` run.

This is the **residual after aliasing** — what survives into the band at the
rate you actually sample. It cannot tell you what folded in; only D can.

## D — scope, 10 MSa/s. The only data that sees above Nyquist.

`D_phase2_*.csv` — columns `t_s, C1_V, C2_V`. 1 000 000 points at 10 MSa/s =
100 ms record, DC–5 MHz. Source: `Experiment_RNoise/out/scope/*.npz`.

| file | C1 probes | condition |
|---|---|---|
| `D_phase2_idle_baseline` | A0 pad (after divider) | un-armed, zero current — reference |
| `D_phase2_supplyA_0p85V` | A0 pad (after divider) | 0.85 V / 186 mA into 4.9 Ω, bench supply A |
| `D_phase2_supplyB_0p85V` | A0 pad (after divider) | same, **different supply** — supply eliminated |
| `D_phase2_ldoOut_0p85V` | **LDO output, before the divider** | same drive, probe moved upstream |

C2 is always the A1 pad (INA296A OUT), no divider, **0.93 V/A measured**.

Four things about these files:

1. **AC-coupled (A1M), 1× probe.** DC is stripped — the volts are ripple only.
   Deliberate: on 8 bits, DC-coupling spends the range on the operating point.
2. **Volts are re-derived from the raw int8 codes at 30 codes/div**, measured,
   not the driver's assumed 25. The `idle_baseline` file had 25 stored and its
   own `_volts` array reads 20 % high; that array is not used here.
3. **C1 on supplyA/supplyB is the node the ADC pin actually sees.** Multiplying
   by 2.10 refers it to the rail *at DC only* — the divider is **uncompensated**
   (÷2.10 at DC, ÷≈1.09 at 24 kHz), so the ADC pin gets half the DC and nearly
   all of the ripple. `ldoOut` is the rail directly, no scaling needed.
4. Measured here: `supplyA` C1 = 78.9 mV rms at the pad (= 165.7 mV rail-referred,
   which is the number in `Experiment_RNoise/STATUS.md` §2.1), C2 = 105.0 mA rms;
   `ldoOut` C1 = 158.3 mV rms directly on the rail.

## E — the signal the filter must not destroy

`E_<level>mA_<heat>ms_c3_joined.csv` and `..._raw.csv`, four conditions:
250 / 550 / 850 mA × 400 ms, plus **850 mA × 100 ms** (the fastest commanded
transient in the campaign — the tightest bandwidth spec). All cycle 3 of
`sweep_20260731_155129` / `sweep_20260731_145838`; cycle 1 is the ramped
bootstrap and is deliberately not used.

`_joined` — all four channels on **one** time base, converted, `t_s = 0` at heat
onset, `phase ∈ {pre, heat, cool}`, columns
`t_s, laser, load, sma_v, sma_i, cc_u, cc_r_est, laser_mm, force_mN, power_W, sma_r, phase`.
Convenient for reading rise times. Streams are **interpolated onto the union
index**, which is a mild smoothing — do not take a spectrum of it.

`_raw` — the same window, long format, **no interpolation and no ZOH dedup**,
one row per sample as the rig wrote it: `t_s, src, stream, value, raw_code, seq`.
Use this for anything spectral. `value` units differ per stream: `src=1/2` volts,
`src=3` volts, **`src=4` amps**, `src=6` volts, `src=7` ohms.

Both have the **M4/M7 clock offset applied** (`src=1/2` shifted onto the M7
clock; untreated, displacement peaks ~2.19 s *before* the current that caused it).

Cross-checked against `cycles.csv` for 850 mA × 400 ms cycle 3:
`dx_um` 6781 vs 6788, `dF_mN` 215.8 vs 216.0, `r_hot` 4.042 vs 4.033.

## Corrections already applied (so you don't re-apply them)

1. **`hw_us` unwrapped per `src`.** It is a 32-bit `micros()` counter, wrapping
   every 4294.97 s. Per-stream, because the streams interleave in the file and a
   global unwrap reads the interleaving as backward jumps.
2. **ZOH duplicates removed on `src=1/2`** (A, B, C `_laser`/`_load` only). The
   M4 polls the data register at ~496 Hz while the ADS1263 converts at 400 SPS,
   so ~19 % of rows re-fetch a value already reported. `n_held` records how many
   raw rows each kept sample stood for (1 = no repeat). Skip this and your
   frequency axis is wrong by ~24 %.
3. **M4/M7 clock offset** applied in E only (A–D are single-clock).
4. **Scope volts re-derived from codes at 30 codes/div** (see D above).

## Things still baked in that you must account for

- **`t_s` is not uniformly spaced** in A/B/C/E-raw — that is the true sample
  instant, kept on purpose. Resample onto a uniform grid (`np.interp`) before
  any FFT; that is what the published figures in
  `noise_20260728_125440_quiet_baseline/make_fig1.py` do, and the RMS values in
  its README are per-segment mean-removed on that grid. A plain `std()` over the
  full record runs ~10 % higher because it also counts 60 s of drift.
- **`n_avg = 4`** conversions averaged per streamed V/I sample in C and E — a
  boxcar, already in the data, and not an anti-alias filter.
- **A0 and A1 are read sequentially**, ~200 µs apart at `ADC_SAMPLES_CYCLE=4`.
  With 24 kHz content on the rail, V and I are sampled at different points of
  the waveform, so their ratio is not a clean R. The anti-alias filter fixes
  this too, by slowing the signals down.
- **`src=3` and `src=4` each read ~+7 % high** from ADC conversion duty. The
  ratio cancels it exactly (`r_ohm` is immune); `p_W` is ~+14 % high.
  Not corrected here, on purpose, so absolute units stay recoverable.
- **Laser and load have no above-Nyquist measurement anywhere in the repo.**
  A/B/C stop at 200 Hz. Sizing an RC for the ADS1263 inputs on evidence needs a
  scope capture at AIN2/3 and AIN4/5 with the rig driving — deferred.
