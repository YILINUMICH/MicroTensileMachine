# Session notes 2026-08-04 — cooling-phase noise, energy definitions, and the NN sampling envelope

Analysis session on `data/raw/sweep_20260731_155129` (24-cell heat-time map, CC fork,
~981 Hz src=3/4 stream) plus the 2026-07-28 `isense_*` bench captures. No code or data
was modified; everything below is measured conclusions and design decisions for the
RNN dataset campaign.

## 1. Cooling-phase noise on the M7 analog channels — confirmed and characterized

Per-sample noise during the 30 s cooling windows (idle bias ~106 mA / ~500 mV):

| channel | idle level | σ (per sample) | relative |
|---|---|---|---|
| sma_i (src=4) | 106 mA | ~24 mA | ~22 % |
| sma_v (src=3) | 500 mV | ~74 mV | ~14 % |
| R = V/I | 4.95 Ω | ~1.4 Ω | ~28 % |

- The front end is **not** noisier during cooling — fire windows show the same absolute σ
  (29 mA / 81 mV). It is a fixed additive broadband floor; the cooling signal is just small.
- **Spectrum**: no dominant tone (largest bin ~2 % of power); ~80 % of power above 120 Hz,
  **< 2 % below 10 Hz**. White-ish: block-averaging scales as √N (25 mA @ 981 Hz →
  2.0 mA @ 10 Hz; R noise 28 % → 2.2 %).
- **V and I noise are uncorrelated** (ρ ≈ −0.002), so nothing cancels in R = V/I.
- Open-circuit floor (`isense_20260728_231141_sma-disconnected-voltage`): σ_I = 12 mA with
  zero current flowing; I-channel offset **+0.44 mA**. V-channel offset still unmeasured
  (that capture had the drive commanding 0.5 V — not a true zero-drive reading).
- **Open-circuit subtraction verdict: no.** The noise is random and incoherent; subtracting
  a separately-recorded trace adds variance (×√2). Only the DC offset (+0.44 mA → 0.4 %
  systematic on R at bias) is worth subtracting.
- **Unexplained 1.7× excess during sweeps**: sweep cooling σ_I = 24 mA vs 14.4 mA in the
  07-28 bench idle capture (`isense_20260728_224340`, same bias, same rate). The floor +
  idle drive explains only ~⅓ of the sweep variance — something active during sweeps
  (camera / stage / armed drive / supply loading) contributes the rest. **TODO: A/B one
  cooling capture with camera+stage off.** Killing it would put 10 Hz-filtered R at ~1.3 %,
  comfortably under the ~3 % transition signal.

## 2. Filtering and order-of-operations rules

- **R: filter first, then divide.** Averaging per-sample R biases it high by ≈ (σ_I/Ī)² ≈
  **+5 % during cooling** (~0.25 Ω of the 4.95 Ω cooling mean is divide bias, not physics).
  Use ~10 Hz zero-phase filtering / block means of V̄ and Ī, segment-wise (gated on the fire
  window). The transition loss (~50–100 ms settle) mostly overlaps the region already
  blanked by the bias-current step artifact.
- **P/E: multiply raw samples, then integrate.** P = v·i is unbiased under uncorrelated
  noise; do **not** compute E from the filtered streams (filter smearing shifts energy
  across the fire/cool boundary).
- Correct offsets **before** the product (they leak into every integral linearly).
- **Integrate trapezoidally on `hw_us`**, not count×Δt: the stream has zero seq drops but
  ~0.12 % of intervals are stretched, up to 125 ms (M7 cooperative-loop stalls).

## 3. Energy: why it is noise-immune, and definitional care

Ê = Σ(V·I)Δt + Σ(V·n_i)Δt + Σ(I·n_v)Δt + Σ(n_v·n_i)Δt. Signal grows ∝ N, zero-mean noise
sums grow ∝ √N → σ_E ≈ 1.7 mJ on a 560 mJ pulse (**0.3 %**), ~3 mJ over a full 30 s cool.
Conditions verified in data: zero-mean-ish (I offset small, V offset TBD), noise
decorrelates fast (<2 % power below 10 Hz), ρ(V,I) ≈ 0. **Integration suppresses noise but
amplifies systematics** (offsets, reference droop, ρ·σVσI — all grow ∝ t):

- Conversion-duty droop **cancels in R but doubles in P** (~+15 % class at n=64). The CC
  loop cannot self-detect it. TODO: one shunt/DMM check during fire → single multiplicative
  calibration on E.
- **Window edges carry more error than noise**: at fire-off P steps ~1.8 W, so ±1 sample of
  boundary jitter = ±1.8 mJ ≈ the entire noise contribution. Define windows from the
  deterministic event/command streams (src 0xF0+ / src 6), never by thresholding current.
- **The idle bias dominates cumulative energy**: 55 mW × 30 s = **1.65 J per cool — 3× the
  650 mA/300 ms pulse (0.56 J)**. Keep separate columns: `E_pulse` (fire-window trapezoid,
  event-bounded — parameter-free, belongs in `analyze_raw.py`) and `E_bias` (following
  cooling interval). Their sum over cycles must equal the whole-record integral (sanity check).

## 4. Leaky integral (thermal-state proxy)

Model: dE_th/dt = P − E_th/τ (lumped: E_th = C·(T−T_amb), τ = R_th·C). Discretize with the
**exact exponential step** (stable on the nonuniform Δt / 125 ms stalls; Euler is not):

    α_k = exp(−Δt_k/τ);  E_th[k+1] = α_k·E_th[k] + P_k·τ·(1−α_k)

- Steady state P·τ (bias floor ~0.11 J at τ=2 s); init at P_bias·τ; propagate across capture
  gaps with one big-Δt step at P_bias (hw_us is monotonic since boot).
- Per-cycle columns: `E_th_start` (= state at fire-on; the covariate for cycle-to-cycle
  drift) and `E_th_end`. Carries the free parameter τ → belongs in the **training-side**
  pipeline, not `analyze_raw.py`.

**Measured τ** (cycle-averaged R(t) decays, this sweep): ≈ **2 s** at 850 mA/300 ms,
stretching to **4–5 s** at 850 mA/400 ms, decay rate *accelerating* late — not a single
exponential. The stretch is transformation physics (latent heat: C_eff = C + L·∂ξ/∂T), so
τ is **state-dependent**, not a function of pulse descriptors. Below 850 mA the decay is
under the noise floor; the earlier "τ ≈ 5 s" was an illustrative placeholder — use **2 s**
as the sub-transition physical anchor. Caveat: R-derived τ conflates R(T, ξ) observation
nonlinearity with thermal dynamics.

**Recommendation for the RNN: a bank of three fixed-τ leaky integrators (0.5 / 2 / 8 s)**
instead of one learned/fitted τ — a basis-function expansion of the memory kernel; no extra
characterization, differentiable, and it hands the network the right timescales. Keep
logging τ_eff per sweep cell as the physics diagnostic. Escalation path if residuals demand
it: nonlinear leak C(T) with a transition bump → two-state (T, ξ) kinetics model.

## 5. Cooling-ΔR detection floor (which cells are above noise)

Test: early-cooling amplitude (R @ 0.3–1 s after off − 20–28 s baseline) vs cycle-to-cycle
σ (~10–25 mΩ). Detected (>3σ, correct sign): ≥850 mA @ 200 ms, ≥750 mA @ 300 ms,
≥550 mA @ 400 ms. **350 mA is invisible at every tested width.** Two nominal low-current
"detections" (250/350 mA @ 400 ms, +30 mΩ) have the wrong sign — bias-step artifact
residue, not signal. The boundary collapses onto delivered pulse energy:
**E_pulse floor ≈ 0.55 J (3σ), comfortable ≥ 0.64 J.** Detected amplitudes at these
energies are mostly the transformation R-drop, not plain TCR — the sub-transition thermal
signal is still below the floor everywhere.

## 6. The NN sampling envelope — energy-first

The envelope is one inequality in **stored energy at fire-off**, not a (I, t) lookup:

    E_stored(I, t) = P(I)·τ·(1 − e^(−t/τ)),   P(I) ≈ 4.4·I²  [W, A]

**Band: 0.6 J ≤ E_stored ≤ 1.2 J** (observability floor with margin ↔ max validated-safe
condition, 850 mA/400 ms ≈ 1.1 J). The upper bound automatically excludes the dangerous
long-hold/high-current corner (950 mA × 1 s would store ~3 J). Consequences at τ = 2 s:
350 mA needs ~1.6 s holds and saturates at 1.1 J; **250 mA saturates at 0.58 J and can
never cross the floor**.

**Sample in (E_stored, P), not (I, t)** — energy is degenerate (many (I, t) per E), and the
NN needs that degeneracy *spanned*, not collapsed: each E delivered both fast (adiabatic)
and slow (lossy) is what teaches the network where the E-collapse fails (the 0.99-vs-1.00
gap). Feasible region: E ∈ [0.6, 1.2] J, P ∈ (E/τ, 4.0 W]; invert via
t = −τ·ln(1 − E/(P·τ)), I = √(P/4.4).

**Firmware idea — fire-to-energy**: the CC fork already streams v·i at ~1 kHz; an
accumulate-and-compare in the fire state terminates at E_target to ~0.3 %, making the rig
enforce the energy envelope directly and demoting the τ model to a reporting role.

**Before the full random campaign — pilot cells at the predicted boundary** (5 cycles each,
~10 min): 350 mA/1.6 s, 450 mA/0.9 s, 550 mA/0.6 s. Validates both the τ = 2 s edge and the
long-hold extrapolation (everything above was calibrated at holds ≤ 400 ms). Keep the
`--i-low 0` passive-cool lesson from this sweep's abort note. Scope note: this is the
**R-observability** envelope; laser/load targets have their own (lower) floors.

## 7. Cooling-phase physical model — apparent-heat-capacity lumped balance (adopted)

Heating and cooling are modeled separately: heating stays the trapezoid energy bookkeeping
(near-adiabatic, pulses ≪ τ); cooling gets a single-body lumped balance with an
**effective heat capacity** carrying the latent-heat bump (A→M re-transformation):

    C_eff(T)·dT/dt = P_bias − h(T)·A·(T − T_amb),   C_eff(T) = m·c + m·L·(dξ_M/dT)

The bump sits on the martensite branch (M_s→M_f), shaped by Liang–Rogers cosine kinetics
or a DSC exotherm (nearly the same curve). This is the established, citable description
(Potapov & Da Silva; Elahinia group's T/stress-dependent heat capacity; Sharar's
temperature-leveling measurements). It reproduces all three observed behaviors with no
fitted parameters: sub-transition it collapses to Newton cooling with τ = mc/hA (the clean
~2 s regime); through the bump the decay slows early then accelerates on exit (the c23
signature); and every parameter is *measured* — c from literature/DSC, L and M_s/M_f from
DSC or the Dynalloy datasheet (~12–28 J/g), hA once from the sub-transition τ. That turns
the measured τ_eff-vs-E_pulse curve from a fitting target into a **prediction to validate**.

**Structural gap — the model lives in T, the data lives in R.** Validation needs an
observation layer: R(T, ξ) = ξ·R_M(T) + (1−ξ)·R_A(T), linear TCR per branch, ρ_M/ρ_A/TCR
from the datasheet, ξ(T) the *same* Liang–Rogers curve already inside C_eff. Adds ~nothing
to tune, but the **mixing rule** (linear-in-ξ vs series/parallel) is a real modeling choice
that scales predicted R(t) amplitude. Hence **two-tier validation**:

1. **Tier 1 — τ_eff vs E_pulse overlay** (timescales ≈ insensitive to mixing rule and
   amplitude calibration; the headline figure).
2. **Tier 2 — full R(t) traces** for three cells (sub-transition / straddling / fully
   through the bump); this is where the mixing rule gets tested.

**Where "zero free parameters" leaks, and the fixes:**

- **Stress shifts M_s/M_f** (Clausius–Clapeyron, ~0.1 K/MPa·σ — several K for a loaded
  coil). DSC/datasheet values are zero-stress; apply the known correction from the load-cell
  reading. Self-check: the E_pulse where τ_eff starts stretching in this sweep *is* a
  measurement of M_s under actual load — if it disagrees with datasheet+shift, believe
  the sweep.
- **h(T)**: free convection ~ ΔT^0.25, so hA calibrated at small ΔT underestimates losses
  at the top of a big pulse by maybe 20–40 %. First cut constant hA; if Tier-1 residuals
  show predicted-too-slow-from-high-T, the ΔT^0.25 law is still parameter-free.
- **P_bias = I²·R(T)** under CC bias — tracks R by a few %; trivial to include since R is
  the predicted quantity anyway.

**Free consistency check**: m·L is order 1–2 J — same magnitude as the 0.55–1.2 J E_pulse
band, so pulses drive *partial* transformation, Δξ ≈ E_available/(m·L). Predicted ΔR should
scale with Δξ; if Δξ at the 0.55 J floor comes out at a few %, it independently explains
where the detection floor sits. Second citable figure for free.

**Discipline rule**: do NOT let L or the mixing rule become fitted parameters at Tier 2 —
the moment one is tuned to make R(t) overlay, τ_eff stops being a prediction and the
citability argument collapses. Show the mismatch and attribute it instead.

**Discretization**: the exact-exponential step does not carry over (it assumed constant τ);
C_eff(T) makes the ODE stiff-ish through the bump. Use RK4 with sub-stepping (through the
bump and across stretched hw_us intervals) on the ~10 Hz filtered grid. The
exact-exponential step stays for the *feature-side* integrator bank — the two live in
different pipelines, and none of this enters `analyze_raw.py`.

## 8. The three cooling "energies" — keep them apart

Only one is a measurement:

1. **E_bias (measurable, parameter-free → `analyze_raw.py`)**: trapezoid of raw v·i on
   hw_us over the event-bounded cooling window (fire-off → next fire-on), offsets corrected
   before the product. Same computation as E_pulse, different window. ~0.3 % noise. The
   only cooling energy computable today with nothing else measured.
2. **E_th(t) (model-derived, training side)**: heat *leaving* the wire is not electrically
   observable — you integrate the §7 ODE forward, driven by measured bias power, from an
   initial T₀ obtained by inverting mc·(T₀−T_amb) + mL·Δξ(T₀) = E_stored at fire-off
   (near-adiabatic pulse + the P·τ·(1−e^(−t/τ)) loss correction). Report `E_sens =
   mc·(T−T_amb)` and `E_lat = mL·(1−ξ_M(T))` separately — the latent piece is the c23
   signature. **Blocked on m, hA, L/M_s/M_f.**
3. **Leaky-integrator bank (feature side, runs today)**: the fixed-τ exact-exponential
   integrators driven by measured P through fire and cool alike — cooling needs no separate
   treatment; the decay falls out of P dropping to bias.

**Physical meaning of the bias**: the ~106 mA / ~0.5 V idle drive exists for R-observability
(zero current → no V → no R), but it is real Joule heating — 55 mW × 30 s = 1.65 J per
cool, 3× a typical pulse. It is the P_bias source term (wire settles toward an elevated
floor, P_bias·τ ≈ 0.11 J at τ = 2 s, ~half-kelvin class), and its fire-off step is the
+9–14 % R artifact that blanks early cooling. Constant *current* bias means the ~0.5 V and
P_bias = I²R drift with R through the cool — which is exactly why E_bias is the trapezoid
of the measured product, not 55 mW × duration.

**Sanity checks tying the three together:**

- Bookkeeping: Σ(E_pulse + E_bias) over cycles = whole-record trapezoid (already planned).
- Physics: per-cycle shed heat = E_th(fire-off) + E_bias − E_th(next fire-on); over steady
  cycling the wire returns to the same state, so shed heat per cycle must equal
  E_pulse + E_bias. Violation beyond droop-class systematics ⇒ hA or C_eff is wrong.
  A self-audit needing no new instruments.

## 9. Practicality revision to §7 — parametrize in what we measure, not in T

M_s/M_f and hA are theoretically measurable but practically hard to settle, and the §7
parameter story ("hA from τ; M_s/M_f from DSC/datasheet") is revised accordingly:

- **hA**: h and A are separately meaningless for a coil — turns share boundary layers,
  h scatters 2–5× in wire-convection literature, A is ambiguous for a helix, and coil
  pitch (hence hA) changes with stroke. But dividing the ODE by mc shows the *dynamics*
  depend only on **τ** (directly measured, ~2 s) and mc enters only as the W→K scale:

      f(T)·dT/dt = P/(mc) − (T − T_amb)/τ,   f(T) = 1 + (L/c)·dξ/dT

  hA never appears as its own object. Its state-dependence is handled empirically: check
  **τ stability across the pilot holds** (different currents/extensions); consistent → the
  lump holds, inconsistent → the dependence has been *measured*, not modeled.
- **M_s/M_f**: they are temperatures, and we never observe temperature. Datasheet values
  are zero-stress lot-nominals; they walk with cold work, spring-forming, and cycling
  history (functional fatigue, several K per campaign), and DSC on a snipped sample
  characterizes the snipped sample, not the trained loaded coil. Flexinol also often cools
  A→R→M through the **R-phase** (second DSC peak, own large resistivity signature), so
  even a good DSC trace doesn't translate cleanly into the R observable.
  **Define the bump in stored-energy coordinates instead**: E_Ms = mc·(M_s − T_amb) is
  directly readable off our own sweep as the E_pulse where τ_eff starts stretching (bump
  exit = where the decay re-accelerates). The sweep is the *primary* measurement; the
  datasheet is the sanity check — the reverse of §7's original framing.

**Revised parameter tiers** (replaces §7's "zero fitted parameters" claim):

| tier | parameters | source |
|---|---|---|
| measured in situ | τ, E_Ms, E_Mf | sub-transition decay; stretch onset/exit vs E_pulse |
| lumped, carried as uncertainty band | mc, L/c | weighed mass × literature c; datasheet L range |
| nominal, consistency-check only | M_s/M_f in °C, ρ_M/ρ_A | datasheet / DSC |
| fitted | — (none) | discipline rule from §7 stands |

Citability claim weakens honestly: two scalar features calibrate two lumps, and the model
then predicts the entire remaining shape (stretch magnitude, exit acceleration, its
E_pulse dependence, Tier-2 traces). Calibration features and validation targets stay
disjoint, so the claim is still non-circular.

## 10. DECISION — the RNN feature vector (for the training side)

Per timestep, on the ~10 Hz grid: **[R̄, P̄, E_th(0.5 s), E_th(2 s), E_th(8 s)]**, plus the
commanded drive state as a sixth input (see below). This makes the training campaign
**independent of every hard-to-settle parameter in §7/§9** — nothing in the vector needs
m, c, hA, L, M_s, or M_f. The three τ's are choices, not measurements; 0.5/2/8 s brackets
the measured 2 s sub-transition decay and the 4–5 s stretched regime. The §7 physics model
is thereby demoted to a diagnostic / paper-figure track; the latent-heat nonlinearity that
makes τ state-dependent is exactly what the network learns from the bank as a basis.

Computation rules (all per §2 — these define the features, follow them exactly):

- **R̄**: block means of V̄ and Ī on the ~10 Hz grid, then divide (filter-first-then-divide;
  segment-wise, gated on the fire window). **Causality flag**: zero-phase filtering is
  fine offline but a deployed model sees a causal filter (~50 ms group delay). Decide up
  front — prefer a causal filter from the start so training and deployment share the same
  operator.
- **P̄**: multiply raw samples, then block-average to the grid (never from filtered streams).
- **E_th(τ) bank**: exact exponential step (§4) on `hw_us` at the full ~981 Hz raw rate,
  driven by raw v·i through fire and cool alike (cooling needs no separate treatment —
  decay falls out of P dropping to bias), then sampled to the 10 Hz grid. Init each
  integrator at P_bias·τ; propagate capture gaps with one big-Δt step at bias power. The
  bank is causal by construction — unchanged if run online later.
- **Drive command input** (src 6 u, or commanded current): technically recoverable from
  I = √(P̄/R̄), but that asks the network to infer through the §1 noise. It's already in
  the stream, it's free, and it lets the network learn "R̄ is untrustworthy ~100 ms after
  a drive step" (the §3/§8 bias-step artifact) instead of being confused by it.
- Correct DC offsets before any product (§2). Windows from event streams only, never
  current thresholds (§3).

**Code location**: this is a training-side dataset builder — it carries free parameters
(the τ's, the filter cutoff) and emits *sequences*, not per-cycle rows, so it does NOT go
in `analyze_raw.py` (NO DATA SELECTION boundary). A separate script (sibling of
`make_rnn_profile.py` in spirit) reads `data/raw`, applies the rules above, writes feature
sequences. `analyze_raw.py` still gains only the parameter-free `E_pulse`/`E_bias`
per-cycle columns (TODO 5, unchanged — per-cycle provenance).

## 11. Data-collection side: frozen

Nothing changes at capture time. Every feature in §10 is computed offline from streams the
CC fork already writes: raw v·i at ~981 Hz (src 3/4), `hw_us`, drive command (src 6),
event markers (src 0xF0+). The τ's, filter choice, and causal-vs-zero-phase decision can
all be changed later and recomputed from the same captures — the payoff of raw-stays-raw.

- What changes is only **what we command**: pilot cells, then the randomized (E, P)
  profile — longer holds at lower currents than the 24-cell sweep (e.g. 350 mA/1.6 s) —
  through the existing profile mechanism. Same capture path, different profile file.
- The three calibration captures (TODOs 1–3) are one-off bench captures, applied in post;
  none block starting the campaign.
- **Fire-to-energy firmware stays optional**: with the open-loop t = −τ·ln(1 − E/(P·τ))
  inversion, delivered energy lands where it lands, and since E_pulse is *measured* per
  cycle the network sees actual, not intended, energy — scatter around the target is
  harmless for training. Build it only if the pilot cells show the τ = 2 s inversion
  missing the energy band badly at long holds.

Rig, firmware, capture format, folder discipline — all frozen. New work = profiles in,
training-side feature builder out.
