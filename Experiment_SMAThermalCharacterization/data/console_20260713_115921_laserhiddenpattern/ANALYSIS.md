# Session `console_20260713_115921` — cycle-aligned analysis

Analysis of the 2026-07-13 SMA thermal console run, visualized against the
actuation pattern it was driven with. Generated by `plot_cycles.py` (added to
`Experiment_SMAThermalCharacterization/` in this session).

## Resistance normalization

Raw ohms aren't comparable across cycles — the pre-fire R wanders between 4.09
and 4.42 Ω. Resistance is therefore plotted as **R/R₀**, with a choice of
reference:

- `--r-ref cold` (default) — R₀ = the session's initial baseline reading
  (4.323 Ω). One reference for the whole run, so the baseline drift stays
  visible.
- `--r-ref cycle` — R₀ = each cycle's own pre-fire R. Drift divided out, so only
  the fire response remains.

`cycles_metrics.csv` carries both (`R_pre_ohm`, `dR_peak_ohm` alongside
`R_pre_norm`, `dR_peak_pct`, `R_end_norm`).

## Findings

**Force is the only trustworthy actuation readout.** σ ≈ 0.4 mN against a
15–30 mN peak swing. It peaks ~0.15–0.25 s *after* the fire ends (thermal lag)
and ratchets upward across the run rather than fully relaxing during the 3 s
cool.

**The displacement spike at each fire is drive feedthrough, not motion.** The
laser steps +3.2 to +3.6 µm inside *every* fire window and is back to 0 ± 0.3 µm
by +0.25 s — exactly when the force is peaking. Real contraction that shows up in
the force would still be present at the force peak; this isn't. Cycle 7 settles
it: force was 8× larger there, and the fire-window laser step was an unchanged
3.24 µm. It tracks the 0.7 A drive, not the mechanics. `plot_cycles.py` tests for
this automatically (`dDisp_in_fire_um` vs `dDisp_at_Fpeak_um`) and labels the
panel when it triggers.

**The 100 ms fire is not resolved in resistance, normalized or not.** ΔR/R₀ is
+7.9 ± 6.5% against a ±12.2% (2σ) noise floor — every point of the ΔR trend sits
inside its own scatter. `sma_r` runs at 49 Hz (≈5 samples inside a 100 ms fire)
with σ ≈ 0.26 Ω on a 4.3 Ω coil. Getting a resistance signature needs a longer
fire, a slower/averaged R stream, or a quieter sense path. The ±2σ band is drawn
on those panels so the trend can't be misread as real.

**Cycle 7 is a discrete mechanical event.** Peak force 0.137 N vs ~0.019 N on
cycles 1–6; cycles 8–10 then sit on a new higher plateau (~0.028 N). Looks like
slack takeup or a grip slip — worth knowing before reading the run as clean.

## Caveats

- **Cycle 1 has no pre-fire baseline.** The SMA V/I/R stream only starts when the
  driver arms, which *is* the first fire. Its `R_pre_*` / `dR_peak_ohm` entries
  are `nan` and the plots label the gap.
- Noise floors are estimated as σ = std(diff(y))/√2 over the cool stretches, not
  a plain std: the force ratchets upward across the session, and a plain std
  would charge that real drift to the noise budget and hide a signal that is in
  fact ~80× its own scatter.
- The laser's absolute displacement carries a large meaningless offset (it sits
  far outside its calibrated span), so it is only ever plotted as a delta.
